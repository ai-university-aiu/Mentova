Accomplishment 372: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 74 - Hole Census Tile-Shape Pairing

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 8698868d with a new glass-box rule called
hole_census (WP-332, Layer 307), raising the benchmark score from
70/120 = 58.33% to 71/120 = 59.17%.

The input grid holds equally sized large tiles of distinct colors packed
side by side, plus an equal number of smaller rectangular shapes
scattered over the background. Both are pierced by background-colored
holes: the tiles carry a few scattered single cells, the shapes carry
hole patterns that clump into blocks, bars, and diagonals. In the output
the scattered shapes are gone and the tile layout alone remains, each
tile redrawn solid with one small shape stamped dead center inside it,
the shape's holes filled by the tile color.

The key insight is that the tiles' holes are a census. A tile with three
holes belongs to the shape whose background-hole region forms exactly
three 4-connected components. Two simpler readings both fail: counting
raw hole cells breaks because a shape's nine-cell hole block is one
component, not nine, and matching hole positions breaks because the tile
holes sit at arbitrary spots. Only the component count of the holes
survives the trip from shape interior to tile face. The pairing is
verified as a bijection - exactly one census match per tile, every shape
used exactly once - so any ambiguous grid becomes a clean rule failure
instead of a wrong stamp.

The rule carries no learned parameters: background color, tiles, shapes,
censuses, pairings, and stamp positions are all derived from the input
grid itself. Both training pairs and the hidden test case passed on the
first run. Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and
glass-box.
