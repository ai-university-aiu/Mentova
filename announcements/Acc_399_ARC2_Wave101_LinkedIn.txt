Accomplishment 399: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 101 - Legend Snake: Pattern-Programmed Paths Climbing a Two-Region Boundary

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 195c6913 with a new glass-box rule
called legend_snake (WP-359, Layer 334), raising the benchmark
score from 97/120 = 80.83% to 98/120 = 81.67%.

Each input of this task shows two big regions of two colors meeting
along a jagged diagonal boundary. Near the top-left corner sits a
legend: a strip of small two-by-two color blocks that spells out a
sequence - say 1, 2, 8, 3. Somewhere else sits one lone block of an
extra color. And on the left edge, single dots of the legend's
first color wait inside one of the regions. In the output every dot
has fired a snake: a path marching east through its own region,
painted with the legend sequence repeating over and over. Each time
the snake hits the other region, the blocking cell takes the lone
block's color and the snake turns - north, then east again at the
next wall - climbing the diagonal boundary like a staircase. The
legend and the lone block vanish, absorbed into the background.

The rule reads everything off the grid at solve time. The two
region colors are simply the two most frequent colors. The
two-by-two blocks of any other color are collected; the ones
sharing a row are the legend, the leftover names the turn color.
Each snake starts at its dot as position zero of the cyclic
pattern and walks, reading the untouched input for navigation while
painting the working copy.

The trick that cracks the task is the turn bookkeeping. A turn mark
spends no position of the pattern, so the color sequence flows
unbroken around every corner of the staircase - verified position
by position across all six snakes of the four grids. And when a
snake turns straight into another wall, it stops right there after
a single mark: one training snake dies against the boundary exactly
this way, and the rule reproduces it.

The complete rule was written in plain English covering every grid
before any code, and a full cell-level diff accounted for every
painted path cell, every turn mark, and every erased legend cell by
hand - down to the pattern position of each painted cell. The
integrated rule passed the single-task test and the full regression
with one trivial fix. Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is
named and glass-box.
