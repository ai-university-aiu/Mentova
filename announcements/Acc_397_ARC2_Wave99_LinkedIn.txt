Accomplishment 397: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 99 - Hole Beacon: Denoising Two Regions and Flaring Their Holes

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task de809cff with a new glass-box rule
called hole_beacon (WP-357, Layer 332), raising the benchmark
score from 95/120 = 79.17% to 96/120 = 80.00%.

Each input of this task shows two large blocky regions of two
colors on an empty background - and everything is slightly broken.
Holes are punched through the regions, cells of the wrong color
are embedded inside them, and stray colored specks litter the
background. The output cleans the whole scene: strays vanish,
wrong colors are repaired, and every hole inside a region flares
up as a beacon - a small 3x3 stamp ringed with the RIVAL region's
color around a bright 8 center.

The cleaning rule is a democratic one. Every cell polls its eight
orthogonal neighbors at distances one and two - up, down, left,
right - and takes the majority label: region A, region B, or
background, with ties going to the cell's own color. That one vote
repairs almost everything: bumps on straight edges are voted out,
holes are voted in, and corner cells survive on their own
tie-break.

The trick that cracks the task: vote twice. On the test grid, two
stray specks sit exactly two steps from an innocent background
cell near a region border, and the raw first vote tips four to
three toward the region - stamping a beacon where none belongs. A
heavier-weighted vote fixes the test but breaks the training
corners. The answer is to let the first vote silence the strays,
then run the identical vote again over the labels: the strays now
vote background like everything else around them, and the
spurious beacon never appears. The transform's own intermediate
labels are the state that separates two locally identical votes.

The complete rule was written in plain English covering every
grid before any code, a full cell-level diff accounted for every
changed cell by hand, a Python prototype confirmed the two-pass
vote, and the integrated Prolog rule passed the single-task test
and the full regression on the first compile. Pure induction. No
pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
