Accomplishment 368: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 70 - Shape Catalogue Recoloring

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 67e490f4 with a new glass-box rule called
shape_catalog (WP-328, Layer 303), raising the benchmark score from
66/120 = 55.00% to 67/120 = 55.83%.

The input grid is dominated by one large lattice block built from
exactly two colors - a frame color drawn over the background - whose
lattice encloses small pockets shaped like squares, dominoes, pluses,
and L-triominoes. Scattered outside the block sits a catalogue of small
shapes, each drawn in its own color. The output is the block alone,
cropped along its bounding box, with every pocket recolored to the
color of the same-shaped catalogue entry.

The key insight is that the outside shapes are a color dictionary keyed
by shape. Every pocket inside the block looks itself up in that
dictionary and paints itself with the color it finds there. The lookup
must be invariant under rotation and reflection: the hidden test block
holds a horizontal domino whose only catalogue mates are drawn
vertically, and the block corners repeat one L-triomino in four
orientations while the catalogue draws it in fewer, so each shape is
reduced to the lexicographically smallest of its eight dihedral-four
variants before comparison.

One more subtlety made the rule honest. The assignment is a vote, not a
first match: one training motif matches catalogue squares in three
different colors at the same time, and the correct color is the one
holding the most catalogue entries. The two-color bounding-box guard
also doubles as catalogue separation, guaranteeing every other
component stands outside the block with no positional filtering.

Both training pairs and the test case passed on the first full
prototype run. Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and
glass-box.
