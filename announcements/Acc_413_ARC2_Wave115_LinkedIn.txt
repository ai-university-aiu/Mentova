Accomplishment 413: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 115 - Pinwheel Spiral: Four Clockwise Arms From One Plus Seed

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task da515329 with a new glass-box rule,
pinwheel_spiral (WP-373, Layer 348), raising the benchmark score
from 111/120 = 92.50% to 112/120 = 93.33%.

The input shows almost nothing: a single small plus sign of one
color, floating on an empty background. The output is a dense,
whole-grid spiral in that same color. The whole task is the
question - how does the tiny seed become the huge spiral?

Read the plus as a machine. It is a pinwheel hub with an empty
center and four equal arms. Each arm is the starting tail of a
square spiral that winds outward, always turning clockwise. The
four spirals are ninety-degree rotations of one another, so their
walls mesh like the teeth of four gears and leave exactly a
one-cell gap everywhere - a period-two pinwheel that fills the
whole board.

Everything hinges on one hidden number: the arm length L, the
distance from the center to a tip. Three lengths follow from it -
the outward run max(1, L-1), a sideways jog max(0, L-2), and a
trailing run max(2, L) - while the leading run of each spiral
layer grows by four, giving 2, 6, 10, 14, and so on. Each arm is a
turtle that walks out to its tip, turns clockwise, and repeats
those layered runs until it leaves the grid.

The discriminator is subtle and beautiful. Because the jog
max(0, L-2) is zero when L is 1 or 2, the two smaller training
pairs have perfectly straight spiral sides and look like an
ordinary spiral - a naive model of runs 1, 4, 8, 12 reproduces
them and hides the real law. Only the L = 3 training pair reveals
that the spiral sides are stepped, carrying a one-cell jog and a
trailing run that grows with L. The unified turtle is the single
law that reproduces all three training pairs AND the held-out
test, whose plus has arm length 4 and whose spiral sides jog by
two.

Clipping is free: the four turtles record every cell they visit,
including cells off the edge, and the renderer simply paints the
in-grid ones. No existing predicate was touched; a full 120-task
regression shows net plus one with zero regression.

The lesson: a whole complex figure can be the deterministic
unfolding of one seed under one parameter, and the parameter can
stay invisible in the easy examples until a single harder example
forces it into the open. Pure induction. No pretraining. Every
rule is named and glass-box.
