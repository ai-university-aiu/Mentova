Accomplishment 391: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 93 - Demo Transfer: Learning a Transformation from a Worked Example inside the Grid

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 4c7dc4dd with a new glass-box rule
called demo_transfer (WP-351, Layer 326), raising the benchmark
score from 89/120 = 74.17% to 90/120 = 75.00% - three quarters of
the benchmark now solved.

Every input of this task is a 30x30 scene holding four small boxed
grids. One box is empty: the answer slot. Two boxes contain the
same colors and form a worked example - the before and the after
of some transformation. The fourth box, in different colors, is
the question. The answer is the demonstrated transformation
applied to the question box.

Here is the twist: the demonstrated transformation changes from
grid to grid. One training scene teaches connect - join the marker
cells with straight filled lines. The other teaches complement -
swap the empty cells and the marked cells. And the test scene
teaches ripple - a small radial pattern around the center moves
outward and repeats like a wave. Ripple appears in no training
answer at all, so any solver that memorizes what the training
pairs did is guaranteed to fail the test. The rule must do what a
human does: look at the worked example inside the grid, understand
the transformation it demonstrates, and transfer it to the new
problem in new colors.

The rule finds the four ring boxes, separates demo from question
by color grouping alone, checks a small library of operations
against the demonstration in both directions, and prefers the
direction whose source looks most like the question. The learned
operation is then applied to the question box with the question's
own colors.

The complete rule was written in plain English covering every grid
before any code, and the integrated rule passed the single-task
test and the full regression on the first compile. Pure induction.
No pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
