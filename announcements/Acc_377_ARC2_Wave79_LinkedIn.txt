Accomplishment 377: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 79 - Master Fragment Location by Isometry Search

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 269e22fb with a new glass-box rule
called master_fragment (WP-337, Layer 312), raising the benchmark
score from 75/120 = 62.50% to 76/120 = 63.33%.

Every training output of the task is one shared canonical two-color
20 by 20 master pattern, reproduced up to one of the eight dihedral
isometries - identity, three rotations, two axis mirrors, and two
diagonal mirrors - and one of the two possible two-color bijections.
Every input is a contiguous rectangular fragment cut directly from
its own output. The answer is the full master pattern rendered in
the fragment's orientation and colors.

The engine stores the first training output verbatim as the
canonical master, then enumerates the eight isometries of the master
crossed with the two color bijections onto the fragment palette -
sixteen full-size candidates in all. A candidate survives only when
the fragment appears inside it as an exact contiguous subgrid, and
after deduplication exactly one candidate must remain: that unique
survivor is the answer, so an ambiguous fragment can never produce a
guessed output.

The key insight is a grid-valued rule parameter. This is the first
wave whose induced rule carries a whole learned grid as a rule
argument - master_fragment(Master) - rather than colors or scalar
parameters, extending the platform's parameterized-rule machinery
from scalars to grids. The uniqueness demand doubles as both the
correctness proof and the induction verifier: each of the five
training pairs admits exactly one candidate among sixteen, so the
rule is self-checking, and the same check answered the hidden test
case on the first complete prototype run with zero bugs fixed. Pure
induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
