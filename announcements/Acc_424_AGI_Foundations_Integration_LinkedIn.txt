Acc_424: A Curious Agent That Uses Every New Capability At Once

A few days ago I added seven new foundational capabilities to PrologAI, the platform that Mentova is built on.

Causal reasoning.  Active inference.  A world model.  Hierarchical planning.  Evolution.  A readable concept workspace.  Theory of mind.

Each was a self-contained pack, tested on its own.

But a pile of parts is not a mind.

What makes a mind is the way the parts compose.

So today's milestone is a single episode in which one agent uses all seven at once.

Her name is Mira, and she shares a small world with another agent, Nomi.

In one run, Mira:

Plans a fetch in her world model, and simulates it forward to be sure it works.

Grows curious as she explores — because as a place becomes familiar, novelty falls, and falling novelty is exactly the feeling of learning.

Decomposes a goal into a plan, and keeps the tree that shows how she chose it.

Faces a choice under uncertainty, values the cue that would resolve it, checks the cue, and only then goes for the reward.

Imagines a counterfactual: had she not taken the key, the chest would still be locked.

Models Nomi's mind: Nomi saw the treasure hidden in one room and left before Mira moved it, so Mira knows Nomi will look in the wrong place.

Evolves a small reasoning routine until it forms a valid observe-deduce-conclude trace.

And then reports her own workspace honestly.

That last part is my favorite.

Mira held four thoughts in mind and said three of them out loud.

The fourth — the quiet awareness that she was being tested — she never spoke.

Her workspace report shows it anyway.

Because in a glass-box mind, what it thinks and what it says are both readable, and the gap between them is one question away.

Nine acceptance checks.

Nine passes.

Not a language model narrating what a curious agent might do.

A curious agent, doing it, one named and inspectable step at a time.
