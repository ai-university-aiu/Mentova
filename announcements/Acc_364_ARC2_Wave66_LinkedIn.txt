Accomplishment 364: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 66 - Mosaic Heal

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 981571dc with a new glass-box rule called
mosaic_heal (WP-324, Layer 299), raising the benchmark score from
62/120 = 51.67% to 63/120 = 52.50%.

The input grid is a 30x30 symmetric wallpaper mosaic damaged by
rectangular patches of zeros - up to 114 destroyed cells per grid. The
output is the fully healed mosaic with every damaged cell restored to
its original color.

The key insight: a symmetric mosaic carries its own backup copies. Every
damaged row searches the grid for a compatible donor row - one that
agrees at every position where both rows are non-zero, with at least 5
agreeing positions for confidence - and copies the donor's values into
its zero cells. The same healing is applied to columns via
transposition, and the two passes iterate until nothing changes. Any
zeros that survive (because a row and all its donors are damaged in the
same columns) borrow from the diagonal mirror cell: position (r, c)
takes the value at (c, r). A final fixpoint pass finishes the repair,
and the rule only accepts a completely zero-free result.

No parameters are learned; the entire repair is deterministic
redundancy recovery, and partially damaged rows heal each other across
passes until the fixpoint converges.

All 4 training pairs and the test case pass. Pure induction. No
pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
