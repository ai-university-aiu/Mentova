Accomplishment 380: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 82 - Twin Lift: Twin-Sized Shapes Rise, Lone Shapes Sink

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 62593bfd with a new glass-box rule
called twin_lift (WP-340, Layer 315), raising the benchmark score
from 78/120 = 65.00% to 79/120 = 65.83%.

Each grid scatters a handful of small shapes over a uniform
background. In the output some shapes have floated straight up until
they touch the top edge and the rest have sunk straight down until
they touch the bottom edge, every shape keeping its columns, its
form, and its colors. Which way does a shape go? The engine counts
the cells of every shape: a shape whose cell count matches the cell
count of at least one other shape rises, and a shape whose cell
count is unique among all shapes sinks. Twins float, loners sink.

The key insight came from the hidden test grid. The reference
description of the task speaks of ranking colors by dominance, but
the test grid holds two shapes of the same color that move in
opposite directions - a 12-cell ring that sinks and a 7-cell
staircase that rises - so no per-color rule can be right. Checking
every candidate mechanism against all fifteen shapes visible across
the three grids eliminated positional, symmetry, density, and
protrusion stories one by one and left exactly one survivor: the
twin-size census. Two training pairs are enough when the invariant
must hold for every shape.

The rule has no learned parameters. The background is the most
frequent color, the shapes are 8-connected components, the census
and both shift targets are computed from the grid at answer time,
and induction is pure verification of both training pairs. Pure
induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
