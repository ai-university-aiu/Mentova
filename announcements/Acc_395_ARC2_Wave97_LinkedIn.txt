Accomplishment 395: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 97 - Hub Dock: Quarter-Turning Loose Shapes onto a Compass Hub

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 2c181942 with a new glass-box rule
called hub_dock (WP-355, Layer 330), raising the benchmark score
from 93/120 = 77.50% to 94/120 = 78.33%.

Each input of this task shows a small compass hub - an eight-cell
ring whose four sides carry two centered cells each, every side in
its own color - surrounded by loose single-color shapes scattered
across the grid. The answer clears the board, keeps the hub where
it stands, and parks every shape whose color matches a hub side
flush against that side, rotated to fit; shapes whose color matches
no side simply vanish.

Two things make it tricky. First, the docking orientation is a
genuine unknown: in the very first training example one shape docks
on the left by a clockwise quarter turn while its neighbor docks on
the right by a counterclockwise quarter turn, so no fixed rotation
works. Second, a plain touch test admits wrong fits: one shape owns
a short side whose two cells would happily touch the hub edge, yet
the correct fit uses a different side. The solver requires the
hub-facing side of the shape to hold exactly two adjacent cells
centered along that side - a condition that rejects the full-length
sides and the off-center sides, and pins both the rotation and the
translation uniquely.

The rule segments the scene, recognizes the hub as the only
component mixing four colors, reads the four sides with their edge
cells, rebuilds the canvas, and docks each matching shape by trying
the four quarter turns until the centered-contact side faces the
hub. The test grid docks four shapes on four different sides - two
by clockwise turns and two by counterclockwise turns - and drops a
fifth, and the same six-stage procedure reads it correctly, cell
for cell.

The complete rule was written in plain English covering every grid
before any code, the Python prototype passed all four grids on the
first run, and the integrated Prolog rule passed the single-task
test and the full regression on the first compile. Pure induction.
No pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
