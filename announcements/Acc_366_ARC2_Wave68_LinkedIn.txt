Accomplishment 366: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 68 - Diagonal Corridor Fill

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 7666fa5d with a new glass-box rule called
diag_corridor (WP-326, Layer 301), raising the benchmark score from
64/120 = 53.33% to 65/120 = 54.17%.

The input grid holds slanted diagonal chains of colored cells - the two
banks of a winding corridor. The task: flood every background cell that
lies between a pair of opposing banks with red (color 2), leaving the
banks and all unbracketed background untouched.

The key insight is a change of coordinates. In ordinary row-column
space the corridors wind and the fill looks irregular - a cell touching
a bank may stay empty while a farther cell turns red. But in the
rotated system s = row + column, d = row - column, every chain becomes
a straight axis-aligned segment, and each corridor becomes a plain
rectangle: line sum strictly between the two banks, cross coordinate
inside the overlap of the two banks' spans. The winding corridor is
straight when viewed along the diagonals.

Two subtleties made the rule honest. All pairs of chains contribute
corridors, not just neighboring pairs - some cells are bracketed only
by the outermost banks. And two distinct chains can sit on the same
diagonal line at opposite ends of the grid, so chains are separated by
true diagonal connectivity, never merely grouped by line position.

No parameters are learned; the entire fill is deterministic geometry in
a rotated coordinate frame.

Both training pairs and the test case pass. Pure induction. No
pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
