Accomplishment 382: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 84 - Motif Shadow: Marker-Taught Rays Cast from Anchored Motifs

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 64efde09 with a new glass-box rule
called motif_shadow (WP-342, Layer 317), raising the benchmark score
from 80/120 = 66.67% to 81/120 = 67.50%.

Each grid holds up to four rectangular motifs, two cells wide and
seven or nine cells long, plus exactly three lone marker cells on
the grid edges. Every motif carries a run of color 2 along one long
side, and the far end of its long axis - the end away from that run -
is its anchor. Each marker aligns with one motif and teaches a
lesson: paint a ray of my color at my offset from the anchor end.
Every motif then casts all three rays sideways, from its exit side
out to the grid edge, over background cells only.

The key insight was the exit direction. In the first training grid
every motif fires its rays on the same side as its color-2 run; in
the second training grid and in the hidden test, every motif fires
on the opposite side - and nothing in the motifs themselves says
which, because even their color palettes change from grid to grid.
The markers break the tie: each marker lies on the ray of its own
color, so its side of the aligned motif reveals the polarity of the
whole grid. What looked like an inconsistent rule became a single
parameter read off the input at answer time.

The rule has no learned parameters. The background is the most
frequent color, the motifs, anchors, lessons, and polarity are all
found in the grid at answer time, and induction is pure verification
of both training pairs. Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule
is named and glass-box.
