Accomplishment 385: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 87 - Twin Toggle: Redrawing Every Shape as Its Other Variant

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task c7f57c3e with a new glass-box rule
called twin_toggle (WP-345, Layer 320), raising the benchmark score
from 83/120 = 69.17% to 84/120 = 70.00%.

Each grid holds several shapes on a uniform background, and every
shape is an integer-scaled copy of one of exactly two variant
templates. The two variants share an identical body of same-colored
cells - a flower of 1s, an X with a fixed center - and differ only
in their decoration: a stem and base drawn in two colors versus one,
or a single marker attached below one twin and above the other in a
different color. The answer redraws every shape as the other
variant at its own scale, positioned so the shared body cells stay
exactly where they were.

The key insight is that the surface reading - three color roles
with an adjacency test choosing between a swap and a promotion - is
a mirage. Dumping all six grids and writing the rule in plain
English before coding revealed one uniform mechanism instead: crop
each shape onto a clean canvas, fully reduce it by the largest
uniform block scale to a canonical template, demand exactly two
distinct templates in the grid, align them by their best
same-colored overlap, and toggle every shape to its twin. Recolored
stems, markers that jump from below to above while changing color,
and a test that does both at once at scales one and three all fall
to the same short procedure, with scale handling coming free from
the block reduction.

The rule has no learned parameters. The background, the components,
the templates, their scales, and the alignment are all read off the
input grid at answer time, and induction is pure verification of
both training pairs. Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is
named and glass-box.
