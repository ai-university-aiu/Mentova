Accomplishment 352: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 54 - Noise Erase

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 71e489b6 with a new glass-box rule called
noise_erase (WP-312, Layer 287), raising the benchmark score from
51/120 = 42.50% to 52/120 = 43.33%.

The input is a binary grid (0s and 1s). Two kinds of noise must be handled:
stray-0 cells (0-clusters embedded inside the 1-region) and stray-1 cells
(isolated 1s inside the 0-region). The rule surrounds each stray-0 cell with
a 3x3 frame of 7s while keeping the stray-0 center as 0, and erases each
stray-1 cell to 0.

The key insight: detecting stray-0 clusters requires a two-phase approach.
Phase 1 seeds are 0-cells where the 1-neighbor count meets or exceeds min(3,
available neighbors), adapting the threshold for edge and corner cells. Phase 2
BFS-expands from seeds to adjacent 0-cells with >=2 1-neighbors, catching
cluster members that individually have only 2 1-neighbors but are reachable
from a seed.

All 3 training pairs and the test case pass. Pure induction. No pretraining.
Every rule is named and glass-box.
