Accomplishment 381: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 83 - Stripe Cycle: A Six-Row Palette Anchored at an Edge Marker Bar

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 221dfab4 with a new glass-box rule
called stripe_cycle (WP-341, Layer 316), raising the benchmark score
from 79/120 = 65.83% to 80/120 = 66.67%.

One edge row of each grid carries a horizontal bar of color 4, and
the bar's columns are the stripe columns. The output repaints every
row by a palette that repeats every six rows: at cyclic distance 0
or 2 from the marker row the stripe columns turn 4, at odd distances
they turn background, and at distance 4 they turn 3 - green - while
every shape cell in that row outside the stripes is overlaid with
green as well. Everything else stays exactly where it was.

The key insight was the anchor. Both training grids put the marker
bar in the bottom row at row 28, and 28 mod 6 equals 4, so a palette
counted from row zero reproduces both training outputs perfectly -
and still fails the hidden test, where the bar moves to the top row
and a row-zero count would paint the marker itself green. The cycle
must be anchored at the marker bar, not at the top of the grid. When
two readings tie on the training pairs, the test input often breaks
the tie; reading every visible grid before coding turned a trap into
a first-compile pass.

The rule has no learned parameters. The background is the most
frequent color, the marker bar and stripe columns are found in the
grid at answer time, and induction is pure verification of both
training pairs. Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named
and glass-box.
