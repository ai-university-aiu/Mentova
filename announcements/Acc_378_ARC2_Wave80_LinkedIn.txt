Accomplishment 378: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 80 - Ring Dock: Sideways Ring Parking with Per-Side Collision

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task b5ca7ac4 with a new glass-box rule
called ring_dock (WP-338, Layer 313), raising the benchmark score
from 76/120 = 63.33% to 77/120 = 64.17%.

Each grid scatters six to eight five-by-five ring objects over a
solid background - every ring is a one-cell uniform border of color 8
or color 2 around a solid three-by-three interior of another color.
In the output every 8-bordered ring has slid horizontally into a
parking lane against the left edge and every 2-bordered ring against
the right edge, each ring keeping its rows exactly. Same-side rings
whose rows overlap pack side by side, and rings starting on the wrong
side of the grid cross the whole board to reach their lane.

The engine detects the rings by scanning for the border-plus-interior
signature under a total-coverage guard - exactly twenty-five
non-background cells per ring, nothing else in the grid - then learns
which border color goes left and which goes right by trying both
assignments against the training pairs. Each side simulates on its
own fresh canvas: movers sorted edge-nearest first slide one column
at a time until the grid edge or an already-parked same-side ring
blocks them, and the two canvases merge into the output.

The key insight is that collision must be scoped to destination. The
first prototype slid every ring over one shared board and failed:
a right mover was blocked by a left mover sitting in its path, even
though the training outputs show the two groups passing straight
through each other as they trade sides. One canvas per side, merged
afterward, fixed all three training pairs at once, and the hidden
test case passed on the same run. Pure induction. No pretraining.
Every rule is named and glass-box.
