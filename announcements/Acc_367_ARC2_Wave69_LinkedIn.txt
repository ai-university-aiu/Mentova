Accomplishment 367: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 69 - Frame Compass Rotation

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task fc7cae8d with a new glass-box rule called
frame_compass (WP-327, Layer 302), raising the benchmark score from
65/120 = 54.17% to 66/120 = 55.00%.

The input grid carries noisy border lines on all four sides, each side
owning a majority color, plus one large patterned component in the
interior hugged by exactly two straight frame lines. The output is the
component cropped out and reoriented - a different rotation or mirror in
every training pair, and yet another in the hidden test case.

The key insight is that the frame lines are a color-coded compass. Each
frame line is painted in the majority color of one grid border side, and
it commands: this edge of the crop must face that border side in the
output. Two adjacent-edge constraints pin down exactly one of the eight
dihedral-four transforms - identity, three rotations, four reflections -
so the orientation is read off the grid, never guessed. The published
human-steps heuristic for this task ("rotate a quarter turn, mirror if
the dominant color leans right") cannot solve the hidden test case,
which demands a main-diagonal reflection no training pair uses; the
compass reading produces it deterministically.

Two subtleties made the rule honest. The largest colored component in
the grid can be the border noise itself when two noisy border lines
fuse at a corner, so components touching any border are excluded before
taking the largest. And two border sides may share a majority color, so
each frame color must resolve to a unique owning side - and it always
does.

All three training pairs and the test case passed on the first full
prototype run. Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and
glass-box.
