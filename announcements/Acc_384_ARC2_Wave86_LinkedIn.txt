Accomplishment 384: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 86 - Xray Frame: Restoring Rectangle Outlines Hidden Under an Occluding Frame

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task a6f40cea with a new glass-box rule
called xray_frame (WP-344, Layer 319), raising the benchmark score
from 82/120 = 68.33% to 83/120 = 69.17%.

Each grid holds one large rectangular frame with a uniformly filled
interior, plus several partial rectangle outlines drawn outside the
frame. The outlines visibly slide under the frame: their edges
break off at the frame boundary and sometimes re-emerge on the far
side. The answer is an x-ray of the occluded region - the frame
interior with every hidden outline cell restored, as if the frame
were transparent. Each shape's bounding box is completed straight
through the frame, and outline halves split by the frame are
reunited by their shared colors before completion.

The key insight hides in the outlines whose edges alternate two
colors cell by cell. Every visible cell of every such shape obeys a
plain checkerboard parity, and the checkerboard correctly restores
three of the four alternating shapes - but one shape in the third
training pair demands the exact opposite colors in its hidden
cells. Alternation continuation, path alternation, rotation, and
translation all fail on at least one shape. The only reading that
explains every hidden cell is the color-swapped mirror: reflect the
hidden cell to the opposite edge of the shape's own bounding box,
read the visible color there, and swap it within the shape's
two-color palette. Writing out every hidden cell of every training
pair by hand before coding exposed both the trap and its escape.

The rule has no learned parameters. The frame, the fill, the
shapes, their bounding boxes, and every restored color are all read
off the input grid at answer time, and induction is pure
verification of all three training pairs. Pure induction. No
pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
