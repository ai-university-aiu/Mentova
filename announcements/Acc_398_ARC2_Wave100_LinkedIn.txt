Accomplishment 398: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 100 - Corner Glyph: Learning a Corner Decoration and Stamping It at Marked Corners

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 4c416de3 with a new glass-box rule
called corner_glyph (WP-358, Layer 333), raising the benchmark
score from 96/120 = 80.00% to 97/120 = 80.83% - on the one
hundredth wave of the climb.

Each input of this task shows boxes outlined in a border color on
a solid background. One box corner is already dressed up: a small
multi-cell decoration in its own color, different in every grid -
a block with a diagonal tail, a zigzag band, a corner cross, a
hook. Other corners carry just a single colored dot, tucked one or
two cells inside the corner on its diagonal. The output finishes
the job: every dotted corner grows the complete decoration in the
dot's own color, correctly mirrored for its corner, while unmarked
corners stay bare.

The rule learns the decoration fresh from each grid. It finds the
one corner with a full multi-cell decoration and records its cells
in the corner's own frame - offsets measured outward along that
corner's directions - so one canonical shape automatically flips
into place at all four corner types. The shape's most inward
diagonal cell doubles as the marker position: any corner holding a
single color exactly there is asking to be decorated.

The trick that cracks the task: the decorations paint over the very
borders that define the corners. A strict corner detector demanding
clean border lines loses exactly the corners that must be stamped -
on a five-cell box side the template even reaches within arm's
length of the opposite corner. The fix is a tolerant L-junction
test: border arms may carry decoration cells anywhere, must never
carry background, and must keep at least two surviving border
cells each. Verified against all four grids, that test finds every
true corner and not one impostor.

The complete rule was written in plain English covering every grid
before any code, a full cell-level diff accounted for every changed
cell by hand, a Prolog prototype exposed and fixed the strict-arm
bug, and the integrated rule passed the single-task test and the
full regression on the first compile. One hundred waves climbed.
Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
