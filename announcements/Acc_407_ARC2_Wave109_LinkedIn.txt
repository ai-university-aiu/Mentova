Accomplishment 407: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 109 - Pipe Router: A Legend of Turn Chiralities and Four Pipes from a Seed

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task e87109e9 with a new glass-box rule
called pipe_router (WP-367, Layer 342), raising the benchmark
score from 105/120 = 87.50% to 106/120 = 88.33%.

Each input of this task is a two-part scene: a six-row legend
strip of framed color swatches across the top, and a playing
field below with a background, colored rectangular blocks, and
one square seed of 8s. Four 8-pipes exactly as wide as the seed
shoot from the seed in the four orthogonal directions. Each pipe
travels straight, painting over background and crossing other
pipes freely, ends when it leaves the grid, and turns ninety
degrees whenever it meets a colored block head-on. The output is
the field alone, legend dropped, with the whole pipe network
painted.

The delicate part is the direction of each turn. Sixteen
block-hit turns occur across the four grids, and they follow no
fixed chirality, no larger-block-side rule, no nearest-edge rule,
and no path-order story - single-snake readings, legend-order
chains, and wrap-around tunnels all break somewhere. The answer
sits in the legend: each six-wide swatch holds one colored pixel
among its four interior cells, and the side of the swatch that
the pixel occupies is a one-bit instruction - left half means
blocks of that color turn pipes left, right half means they turn
pipes right. All sixteen turns match, sixteen of sixteen. The
test input also grows the seed from 2x2 to 3x3, so the pipe width
is derived from the seed rather than hard-coded.

The wave's lesson: legends can carry per-color programs, not just
colors or orders - and when a painted structure branches from one
point in all four directions, model it as four independent agents
leaving a seed rather than one continuous path.

The complete rule was written in plain English covering every
grid before any code, every 8-cell of all four outputs was
accounted for by hand, and the integrated Prolog rule passed the
single-task test and the full regression on its first compile.
Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and
glass-box.
