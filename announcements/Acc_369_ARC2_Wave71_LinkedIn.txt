Accomplishment 369: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 71 - Wallpaper Motif Self-Placement

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task b99e7126 with a new glass-box rule called
wallpaper_motif (WP-329, Layer 304), raising the benchmark score from
67/120 = 55.83% to 68/120 = 56.67%.

The input grid is a wallpaper: uniform separator rows and columns carve
it into a 7x7 tile grid of 3x3 tiles carrying exactly two designs - a
majority standard tile and a minority motif tile, the same design with
one extra color painted into some of its cells. The output stamps the
motif tile at additional positions in the tile grid.

The key insight is that the motif is its own placement map. The picture
painted in the motif color inside one 3x3 tile is exactly the picture
that the motif tiles must paint across the 7x7 tile grid - a fractal in
miniature. The rule reads that picture as a mask, slides the mask over
the tile grid until every motif tile already visible in the input lands
on a mask position, and then completes the stamp: motif tile at every
anchored mask position, standard tile everywhere else, separator lines
untouched.

One failed prototype taught the honest lesson. Building the mask from
the cell-by-cell difference between the two tile designs passes one
training pair and fails the other two, because the difference mask also
includes the positions where the motif tile paints its complement color.
Only the motif color's own cells form the self-similar placement
picture. A partially passing mask definition is still the wrong
abstraction.

All three training pairs and the test case passed on the first run with
the corrected mask. The rule carries no learned parameters: separator
color, tile designs, motif color, mask, and anchor are all derived from
the input grid itself. Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is
named and glass-box.
