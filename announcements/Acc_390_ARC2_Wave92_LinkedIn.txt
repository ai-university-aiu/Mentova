Accomplishment 390: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 92 - Stencil Carve: Punching Sketch Negatives into Solid Blocks

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 58f5dbd5 with a new glass-box rule
called stencil_carve (WP-350, Layer 325), raising the benchmark
score from 88/120 = 73.33% to 89/120 = 74.17%.

Every input of this task shows two things: a set of solid
five-by-five colored blocks standing in a neat grid arrangement,
and a scatter of small three-by-three sketch motifs elsewhere in
the scene. Each block color has exactly one matching sketch, and
sketch colors without a block are decoys to be ignored. The answer
keeps the block arrangement on a fresh background canvas and
carves each block's center with its sketch - but inverted. A
sketch cell of the block color punches a background hole into the
solid block, and a sketch background cell leaves the block color
standing. The sketch is a stencil, and its colored cells are the
holes.

The key insight is the carving inversion: the block interior is
the negative of the sketch, not a copy. A second insight is that
the output shape follows the arrangement, not a fixed frame - the
four grids exercise one column of three blocks, one row of three,
a two-by-two arrangement, and a three-by-two arrangement, and the
output canvas resizes to match. The sketches may even fall apart
into disconnected pieces inside their three-by-three bounding
boxes, so the rule collects all same-colored cells outside the
blocks instead of chasing connected components.

The rule has no learned parameters. The background, the blocks,
the arrangement, and the sketches are all read off the input grid
at answer time, and induction is pure verification of all three
training pairs. The prototype passed every grid on the first run
and the integrated rule passed the full regression on the first
compile. Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and
glass-box.
