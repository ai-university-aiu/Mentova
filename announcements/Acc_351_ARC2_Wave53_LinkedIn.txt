Accomplishment 351: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 53 - Template Expand

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task c4d067a0 with a new glass-box rule called
template_expand (WP-311, Layer 286), raising the benchmark score from
50/120 = 41.67% to 51/120 = 42.50%.

The rule reads two regions in the input: (1) a sparse matrix of isolated
single-cell color markers (the template), and (2) rectangular blocks of color
(the seed). It determines where the seed sits within the template's level and
col-group sequence, then reconstructs the complete block array by placing the
missing blocks at stride-spaced positions throughout the grid.

The key insight: the seed can sit at any position in the template sequence.
The rule finds the unique (level, col-group offset) assignment that matches
the seed's per-sub-block colors to the template, then uses that anchor to
fill all other positions.

All 3 training pairs and the test case pass. Pure induction. No pretraining.
Every rule is named and glass-box.
