Accomplishment 406: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 108 - Cavity Paint: One Figure Drawn Twice and the Normalized-Centroid Match

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 3a25b0d8 with a new glass-box rule
called cavity_paint (WP-366, Layer 341), raising the benchmark
score from 104/120 = 86.67% to 105/120 = 87.50%.

Each input of this task shows the same figure drawn twice. One
drawing is a hollow silhouette - a closed skeleton in a single
color whose enclosed background pockets are the figure's
cavities. The other is a multi-color template - the same figure
redrawn at a different size, visibly warped, with its cavities
already filled with paint. The output crops to the silhouette,
keeps its cells, and paints every enclosed cavity with the color
of its counterpart region in the template, while open background
stays background.

The delicate part is deciding which template region matches which
cavity. The warp shifts regions off their rows, their columns,
and even their top-to-bottom ranks: in one training pair the
middle cavity starts a row below its flanking cavities while its
template twin starts level with its own flankers, so ordinal
matching paints the wrong color. The invariant that survives the
warp is position proportional to the bounding box - each region's
centroid, normalized to its own shape's bounding box, lands
nearly on top of its counterpart's. That normalized-centroid
pairing lands every color on every cavity across both training
pairs and all nine cavities of the test.

The wave also carried a methodological lesson: this task had been
filed for months under a stale label from an early survey -
mirror-symmetry completion - and a fresh grid dump showed no
mirror axis at all, just a clean two-shape structure with a fully
computable rule. Fresh eyes on the data beat inherited
classifications.

The complete rule was written in plain English covering every
grid before any code, every cell of both training outputs was
accounted for by hand, and the integrated Prolog rule passed the
single-task test and the full regression on its first compile.
Pure induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and
glass-box.
