Accomplishment 379: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 81 - Wire Relay: Tip-to-Tip Color Relay over a Wire Maze

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task a47bf94d with a new glass-box rule
called wire_relay (WP-339, Layer 314), raising the benchmark score
from 77/120 = 64.17% to 78/120 = 65.00%.

Each grid holds a maze of wires drawn in color 8, crossing marks of
color 5 where two wires pass through each other, and solid blocks of
color 9 that wires enter and leave. Every wire runs from a tip
pointing up or left to a tip pointing down or right, and a colored
figure - a solid square, a diamond outline, or a diagonal X - sits
two cells beyond one of the two tips. In the output every wire
carries its figure color to both ends: a diamond outline appears
beyond the up-or-left tip and a diagonal X beyond the down-or-right
tip, while the wire maze itself stays untouched.

The engine finds every tip as a wire cell with exactly one structural
neighbor, then traces each wire cell by cell, always preferring the
straight-ahead cell - which carries the walk through crossings
without switching wires - and turning only into a unique
perpendicular neighbor. A color-9 block is flood filled and jumped:
straight through when a wire waits directly beyond, otherwise to the
block's only other adjacent wire cell.

The key insight is that hidden test grids exercise freedoms the
training pairs leave dormant. The first prototype walked blocks
straight through and passed all three training pairs, but the test
grid hides a corner turn inside one block - a wire enters heading
west and leaves heading south. Modeling the block by its function,
as a connector of its perimeter wires, fixed the test while leaving
every training trace unchanged. Pure induction. No pretraining.
Every rule is named and glass-box.
