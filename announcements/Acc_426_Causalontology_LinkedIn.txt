Acc_426: I Taught an AI to Play a Game by Talking to It - and Every Word Became Logic

A child learns cause and effect by touching the world.

Press the button, the light comes on.  Touch the stove, it hurts.  Do it, watch what follows, remember why.

Most AI never does this.  It reads about the world instead of acting in it, so it correlates without ever truly causing.

Today's milestone is Causalontology: cause and effect as a first-class, learnable, inspectable object, delivered as six new packs on PrologAI and a new application on Mentova.

The heart of it is a reified causal relation.  Not "A relates to B," but: these causes, these effects, this time window, this kind of necessity, this strength, this provenance - who said so, and how it was checked.

Three things I want to show you.

First, timing is mechanism.  Ask a doctor with a food-poisoned patient what they ask first: "What did you eat, and how long ago?"  Mentova now reasons the same way.  Two suspect meals; the shellfish from three hours ago is admitted, the poultry from two hours ago is excluded - purely because two hours is too soon for poultry to act.  A cause ruled out by the clock alone.

Second, "after" is never "because."  The rooster crows before the sunrise, every single day.  Mentova stores that sequence - and can prove to you that it is not causation.

Third, and my favorite: the guided game.

There is a new split screen.  On top, a little locksmith game - a key, a locked door, a hidden trap.  Below it, a chat.

I clicked a cell and typed: "that looks like a key."

I typed: "pick that up."

I typed: "don't touch that" - on the trap.

I typed: "that looks like a door.  Let's walk through it."

Each sentence was grounded into a formal assertion - a labeled object, a raised priority, an enforced hazard, a goal handed to the planner - and routed through the same review machinery human mentors already use to teach Mentova facts.

Nine steps later, Mentova walked through the door.

Then I asked it why.

It told me which of its choices came from my guidance and which it worked out alone.

That distinction - guided versus discovered, on the record, for every action - is how a mind grows out of its teacher honestly.

Eight acceptance checks.  Eight passes.  And the specification's own demonstration program runs verbatim, self-checks green.

The interactive ARC-AGI-3 benchmark, where the strongest language models score zero, is the labeled bet this machinery was shaped for.  A bet, not a claim - and a glass box turns even a failed bet into a diagnosis.
