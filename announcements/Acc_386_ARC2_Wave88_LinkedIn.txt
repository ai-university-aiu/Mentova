Accomplishment 386: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 88 - Band Jigsaw: Restacking Every Shape Flush Against a Ragged Band

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 16b78196 with a new glass-box rule
called band_jigsaw (WP-346, Layer 321), raising the benchmark score
from 84/120 = 70.00% to 85/120 = 70.83%.

Each grid holds one wide band component that spans the full grid
width - or, in the test case, the full grid height - with ragged,
notched edges, plus a handful of small single-color shapes
scattered around it. The answer keeps the band exactly where it is
and restacks every shape flush against it, so the pieces interlock
like a jigsaw puzzle: each chain of shapes exactly fills the space
between the band's ragged edge and a perfectly straight outer line,
plugging the band's notches on the way. Shapes translate only -
they are never rotated, flipped, or recolored.

The key insight is that the straight outer edge is a computed
quantity, not a searched one. Once a column interval is chosen, the
exact-area equation - interval width times outer row minus the
band's profile sum equals the total shape area - forces the outer
edge row, and the region between profile and edge is exact-tiled by
anchored exact cover: the first uncovered cell in row-major order
must host some unused shape's first cell. The human-steps reading
of the task, wide shapes above and narrow shapes below, is wrong in
general - one training pair stacks all three shapes below the band
- so the side split is searched rather than assumed. And because
the test grid rotates the whole scene ninety degrees, the solver
canonicalizes a vertical band by transposition and solves the far
side by vertical flip, handling all orientations with one
mechanism.

The rule has no learned parameters. The background, the band, its
edge profiles, the shape inventory, the side split, and every
placement are all read off the input grid at answer time, and
induction is pure verification of both training pairs. Pure
induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
