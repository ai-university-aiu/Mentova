Accomplishment 365: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 67 - Mirror Patch

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 0934a4d8 with a new glass-box rule called
mirror_patch (WP-325, Layer 300), raising the benchmark score from
63/120 = 52.50% to 64/120 = 53.33%.

The input grid is a 30x30 mirror-symmetric wallpaper in which one
rectangular patch has been covered by a solid block of color 8. The
output is the hidden patch alone - exactly the size of the cover, with
every concealed cell restored.

The key insight: the wallpaper's mirror axes do not sit at the grid
center. The 30x30 grid is an asymmetric crop of the wallpaper, so the
rule detects the true horizontal and vertical reflection axes directly
from the visible cells - a candidate axis is accepted only when no pair
of mirrored visible cells disagrees and more than 50 pairs support it.
Covered cells are then healed to a fixpoint from their horizontal,
vertical, or point mirror images, with healed cells becoming donors for
later iterations.

The test case hides the patch flush against the left edge, where the
columns mirror outside the grid and the central rows mirror into the
cover itself. Eight cells are unreachable by any mirror - and they fall
back to the transpose donor: position (r, c) takes the value at (c, r),
which holds exactly in the wallpaper's central band. The mirror
fixpoint then completes the patch, and the rule only accepts a result
free of 8s.

No parameters are learned; the entire recovery is deterministic
symmetry detection plus redundancy readout.

All 4 training pairs and the test case pass. Pure induction. No
pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
