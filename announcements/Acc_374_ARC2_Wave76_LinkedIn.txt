Accomplishment 374: ARC-AGI-2 Wave 76 - Puzzle Fit Rotation-Only Square Assembly

PrologAI solves ARC-AGI-2 task 7b3084d4 with a new glass-box rule called
puzzle_fit (WP-334, Layer 309), raising the benchmark score from
72/120 = 60.00% to 73/120 = 60.83%.

The input grid scatters four puzzle pieces over the background - mostly
single-color connected shapes with bumps and notches, one of them
carrying a single anchor stud of color 5 in place of one of its own
cells. The total number of piece cells is always a perfect square. The
output is the assembled square: every piece rotated by quarter turns,
the rotated pieces interlocking with no gaps and no overlaps, and the
anchor stud landing exactly on the square's top-left corner.

The key insight is that reflections do not belong to this puzzle.
Allowing all eight dihedral variants per piece makes the task
ambiguous - the grids admit two or three perfect tilings each, and only
one of them is the intended answer. Restricting each piece to its four
quarter-turn rotations collapses every grid, training and test alike,
to exactly one perfect tiling, confirmed by exhaustive enumeration. The
pieces are chiral, and their handedness is part of the answer.

The engine extracts the pieces as 8-connected multi-color components,
normalizes them to their bounding boxes, derives the square's side from
the total cell count, and runs a scanline-anchored exact-cover search:
the first empty board cell must be covered by the first cell of some
rotated piece, which prunes the search to a handful of branches. The
rule carries no learned parameters: background, pieces, square size,
rotations, and the anchored corner are all derived from the input grid
itself. All three training pairs and the hidden test case pass. Pure
induction. No pretraining. Every rule is named and glass-box.
