MENTOVA CHAT - DOCUMENT 1 - SPECIFICATION - VERSION 2


Purpose.


This document states what the Mentova text-only web chat must do.


It is the first of six living documents, and it is updated whenever the code changes.


Scope.


In scope is the typed, two-tier web chat: a read-only public visitor tier and a signed-in mentor tier that can teach through review.


Out of scope for this milestone is all audio, meaning the microphone, the loudspeaker, the spoken voice, and speech recognition, which are deferred by Appendix F of the master plan.


What the feature must do.


A visitor can open the chat page in a browser and hold a typed conversation with Mentova.


Every word Mentova uses is grounded through the Lattice to simpler grounded words or to perception, never to text statistics.


A visitor can ask "why?" about any answer and receive a glass-box justification that unfolds to grounded words with no dead ends.


When Mentova does not know, it says so honestly and never invents an answer.


Focus prosody is shown as a written cue, such as italics or small capitals on the emphasized word.


Emotional Prosody is shown as an honest written tone cue, and is read from the visitor's word choice, punctuation, and emoji.


A mentor can sign in and propose a fact or correction, which enters the Review Queue and only reaches the Lattice once approved, with every step written to the Audit Log.


Every chat on both tiers is logged in full.


The child-safety and content rules are enforced on both what visitors see and what Mentova says.


How we know it is done.


The feature is done when the definition of done and the acceptance conversations in Appendix I of the master plan all pass.


Governing sources.


This document follows the master plan, currently at Version 15 or later, especially Appendix F for scope, Appendix I for done, Appendix H for logging, and Appendix K for child-safety.

VERSION 2 ADDITION - THE FACT REFINERY (Acc_425, 2026-07-10).


The chat's teaching path gains a second proposer beside human mentors: the fact refinery (src/mentova/fact_refinery.pl), implementing Appendices Q, R, and S of Teach_Mentova_To_Chat_v2.


Specification items: candidate facts carry a strict subject-relation-object shape with the exact source sentence and a source(SourceId, LineNo) citation; qualification assigns a grounding kind, direct or testimonial, and a confidence; an odd panel of genuinely different models votes blind with normalized answers, abstentions never counting as disagreement; a plain-code honesty gate confirms concept connection and citation reachability, even for unanimous facts; survivors grade into high, medium, or the Minority Report Bin; surviving facts enter this chat's Review Queue through mc_propose_fact under the reserved system mentor id zero; approved facts ground with full provenance and answer "why?" by citing the record, the votes, and the checks.


The look-it-up library tier (src/mentova/reference_library.pl) is specified as streamed, searchable, and never asserted as fact.
