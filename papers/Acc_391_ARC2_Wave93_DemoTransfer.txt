TASK DESCRIPTION

Task 4c7dc4dd presents two training pairs and one test case. All
three inputs are 30x30 scenes filled with a noisy diagonal color
pattern; the outputs are 5x5, 4x4, and 5x5. Each scene holds four
square ring boxes - complete one-color rectangle outlines sharing
one frame color and one side length - whose interiors rest on a
zero background with sparse colored marks. One interior is entirely
empty: the answer slot. Two interiors share the same set of nonzero
colors and form a worked demonstration, the before state and the
after state of a transformation. The fourth interior, carrying its
own distinct colors, is the question. The output is the
demonstrated transformation applied to the question interior.

The decisive property of this task is that the demonstrated
transformation differs from grid to grid. The first training pair
demonstrates connect: marker cells are joined by straight filled
segments. The second training pair demonstrates complement: zero
and the single mark color swap places. The test grid demonstrates
ripple: a small radial pattern around the center moves outward and
repeats. Ripple appears in no training output, so any solver that
memorizes the training transformation fails the test by
construction. The transformation must be read off the scene at
answer time.

RULE: demo_transfer

The glass-box rule is a five-stage procedure whose parameters are
all read off the input grid at solve time:

1. RING SCAN (dt_ring_/7, dt_boxes_/2)
   The scan finds every complete square ring of one nonzero color
   with side five to nine whose interior holds at most two distinct
   nonzero colors and at least four zero cells. The frame-color and
   side-length group holding exactly four rings yields the four box
   interiors.

2. CLASSIFICATION (dt_classify_/4)
   The all-zero interior is the answer slot and is set aside. Of
   the remaining three, the two interiors sharing one nonzero color
   set are the demonstration pair, and the third is the question.
   The noisy region frames of the scene never need to be parsed:
   color grouping alone separates demo from question.

3. OPERATION LIBRARY (dt_op_/3, dt_apply_/3)
   Three operations are checked against the demonstration pair in
   both directions. Complement swaps zero with the single mark
   color. Connect fills every straight run of zeros between two
   collinear marks with the non-corner mark color, trying each mark
   color as the corner. Ripple reads a Manhattan-distance color
   profile around the center cell, requires leading mark rings
   followed by zero rings, and pushes the pattern outward by its
   own extent, repeating it periodically.

4. DIRECTION CHOICE (dt_learn_/4)
   When both directions match - complement is its own inverse - the
   rule prefers the direction whose source box has a nonzero-cell
   count closest to the question box. Position-based pairing gives
   the wrong direction on the test grid; count matching picks the
   correct source in all three grids.

5. TRANSFER (dt_apply_/3)
   The learned operation is applied to the question interior with
   the question's own colors: the corner color learned from the
   demonstration persists, while the fill color is the question's
   other mark. The transformed interior is the output.

On the test grid the demonstration shows a plus of marks around the
center turning into concentric diamond rings. The ripple operation
reproduces it exactly: the radial profile mark-mark-zero-zero-zero
becomes zero-zero-mark-mark-mark, the pattern pushed outward by its
own extent of two and repeated. Applied to the question box - the
same plus shape in different colors - it produces the expected 5x5
answer cell for cell.
