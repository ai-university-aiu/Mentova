Step 5: heal every leftover blue cell by breadth-first search to its nearest non-blue neighbor, scanned in row-major order.

Step 6: flood-fill the region in front of each stalk on the cleaned grid and paint it with the injection color.

Step 7: rotate the whole board a quarter turn if a seven glyph was found.
