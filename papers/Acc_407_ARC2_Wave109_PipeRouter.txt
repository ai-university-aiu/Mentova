TASK DESCRIPTION

Task e87109e9 presents three training pairs and one test case, on
inputs of 25x24 and 30x24 with outputs of 19x24 and 24x24. Every
input is a two-part scene: a six-row legend strip across the top,
and a playing field below it. The output is the field alone, with
a network of color-8 pipes painted onto it.

The legend strip holds four swatches, each six cells wide: a
frame of 5s around four interior cells, of which exactly one is a
colored pixel. The field holds a majority-color background
(3, 1, 7, and 9 across the four grids - every role is read off
each grid, not memorized), several colored rectangular blocks,
and one square seed of 8s. The training seeds are 2x2; the test
seed is 3x3, so the stroke width of the pipes must be derived
from the seed, never hard-coded.

Four pipes exactly as wide as the seed shoot from the seed in the
four orthogonal directions. Each pipe travels straight, painting
8s over background cells and crossing other pipes freely. A pipe
that reaches a grid edge ends there. A pipe that meets a colored
block head-on stops beside it and turns ninety degrees - and the
direction of that turn is what the legend encodes. A swatch whose
colored pixel sits on the left half of its interior declares that
blocks of that color turn pipes LEFT; a pixel on the right half
declares RIGHT. After turning, the pipe continues from its corner
square, obeying the same rules, until it exits the grid.

The legend handedness is the discriminating observation of the
wave. The sixteen block-hit turns across the four grids follow no
larger-extent rule, no nearest-edge rule, no legend-order chain,
no torus wrap, and no fixed chirality - single-snake readings,
spatial clockwise cycles, and wrap-around tunnels all fail on
some grid. Yet all sixteen turns match the side of the swatch on
which that block color's pixel sits: sixteen of sixteen.

RULE: pipe_router

The glass-box rule is a six-stage procedure whose parameters are
all read off the input grid at solve time:

1. SPLIT (arc2_transform(pipe_router)/3)
   The input divides into the six-row legend strip and the
   playing field. The output has the field's dimensions.

2. LEGEND PARSE (prl_turn_map_/2, prl_swatch_/4)
   Each six-wide swatch is decoded from the second legend row:
   the colored interior pixel (neither 0 nor the frame color 5)
   names a block color, and its interior offset maps that color
   to a turn handedness - offsets 0 and 1 to left, offsets 2 and
   3 to right.

3. SCENE ROLES (arc2_bg_color_/2, prl_seed_/6)
   The majority color of the field is the background. The 8-cells
   form a square seed whose bounding box gives the pipe width.

4. FOUR PIPES (prl_march_/12 launches)
   One pipe per orthogonal direction starts adjacent to the seed,
   as wide as the seed and aligned with the seed's rows or
   columns.

5. MARCHING (prl_march_/12, prl_front_in_/4, prl_front_cells_/4,
   prl_step_/3)
   Each pipe advances one front line at a time, painting its
   cells 8. Background and already-painted pipe cells are
   passable; a front line outside the grid ends the pipe.

6. TURNING (prl_obstacle_/4, prl_turn_/3, prl_corner_/5,
   prl_next_front_/4)
   A front line containing any block cell (neither background nor
   8) stops the pipe. The pipe's head square becomes a corner,
   painted in full, and the pipe turns left or right according to
   the blocking color's legend handedness, then marches on from
   the corner in the new direction. A depth cap of sixteen turns
   guards termination.

VERIFICATION

The complete rule was written in plain English covering all three
training pairs and the test case before any code, following the
proven wave method: all eight grids were dumped to text, every
8-cell of all four outputs was accounted for by hand as ray, turn
corner, or continuation, and all sixteen block-hit turns were
catalogued as independent events and checked against the legend
pixel sides - sixteen of sixteen matched, while every rival
hypothesis (single snake, legend-order chain, torus wrap, spatial
cycle, larger-extent turn, nearest-edge turn) failed at least one
grid. The integrated Prolog rule passed the single-task test and
the full regression on its first compile: induction returns
pipe_router, the test output matches exactly, and every
previously solved task still passes. Induction is pure
verification: a cheap pre-filter (rows one and six of the input
entirely frame color 5, output exactly six rows shorter than the
input at equal width) followed by exact transformation of every
training pair.

A lesson of independent interest: legends can carry per-color
programs, not just colors or orders. The position of a pixel
inside its swatch is a one-bit instruction - turn left or turn
right - and the apparent left-to-right order of the swatches
turns out to be irrelevant. When a painted structure branches
from one point in all four directions, model it as four
independent agents leaving a seed rather than one continuous
path.
