TASK DESCRIPTION

Task f560132c presents 20x20 training grids and a 26x26 test grid, each
scattering four pieces over the background. Three pieces are
single-color connected shapes. The fourth - the key - carries an
embedded 2-by-2 block of four foreign palette colors in place of four
of its own body cells. The total number of piece cells is always a
perfect square - 64 and 100 across the two training grids and 144 in
the test grid. The output is the N-by-N square assembled from the four
pieces: every piece is rotated by some number of quarter turns, the
rotated pieces interlock with no gaps and no overlaps, the key piece
covers the square's top-left corner, and every piece is repainted with
the palette color of the output corner it covers.

RULE: palette_jigsaw

The glass-box named rule is a five-stage assembly-and-recoloring
procedure with no learned parameters:

1. PIECE EXTRACTION (pf_cells_/3, pf_comps_/2, pj_multi_/1)
   The background is the most common cell value. All non-background
   cells are grouped into 8-connected components over mixed colors.
   Exactly four components must emerge, and exactly one - the key -
   carries more than one distinct color.

2. PALETTE READING (pj_majority_/2)
   The key's body color is its most frequent cell color. The four key
   cells whose color differs from the body must form a 2-by-2 block,
   read as top-left, top-right, bottom-left, and bottom-right palette
   colors.

3. INDEXED MASKS (pj_mask_/4, pf_norm_/2, pf_count_/3)
   Each piece is translated to its bounding-box origin and converted
   to a shape mask tagged with a piece index; the key is index 1 and
   its palette cells count as part of its shape. The mask cell counts
   must sum to a perfect square N * N, fixing the output side N.

4. ANCHORED EXACT-COVER SEARCH (pf_solve_/3, pf_fill_/5)
   The Wave 76 exact-cover engine tiles the N-by-N board using the
   four quarter-turn rotation variants of each mask: the scanline-first
   empty cell must be covered by the scanline-first cell of some
   rotated variant of a still-unplaced piece. The accepted tiling is
   the one where the key piece covers the board's top-left corner and
   the four board corners are covered by four distinct pieces.

5. CORNER RECOLORING AND RENDER (pj_color_/9, pf_render_/3)
   Each placed piece is repainted with the palette color of the corner
   it covers: the key takes the palette's top-left color, the piece on
   the top-right corner takes the top-right color, and likewise for
   the two bottom corners. The recolored cells are painted row by row
   into the output square.

THE KEY INSIGHT: THE PALETTE CARRIER ANCHORS THE CORNER

Without any anchoring constraint each grid admits exactly four
distinct-corner tilings - the four global rotations of one and the
same assembly - and only one of them recolors correctly. Pinning the
palette-carrying key piece to the top-left corner, the same corner
where its own 2-by-2 palette reading begins, collapses every grid -
both training grids and the test grid - to exactly one tiling,
confirmed by exhaustive enumeration. This generalizes the Wave 76
anchor from a single colored stud to a role-bearing piece. A second
lesson: the human-solver description of this task (quadrant roles
assigned from centroid offsets, with a fixed rotation per role) does
not survive contact with the data - the piece-to-corner assignment
does not follow the pieces' input quadrants, and the per-piece
rotations vary freely between grids - so the jigsaw search
interpretation is the real rule.

INDUCTION PRE-FILTER

The first training pair's output must have strictly fewer rows and
strictly fewer columns than its input, be exactly square, and carry
four distinct colors in its four corners (puzzle_fit's shape carries
color 5 at the top-left corner and is dispatched first). All training
pairs are then verified with forall before the rule is selected. The
checks are constant-time, keeping the pre-filter cheap for every other
task in the named-rule enumeration. The induce clause dispatches early,
directly after puzzle_fit, ahead of the generic named-rule search.

KEY PREDICATES

  pj_multi_/1          - component carries more than one distinct color
  pj_majority_/2       - most frequent color in a color list
  pj_mask_/4           - normalized piece mask tagged with piece index
  pj_color_/9          - piece index mapped to its corner palette color
  pf_cells_/3          - non-background cells as row-column-color (reused)
  pf_comps_/2          - 8-connected mixed-color components (reused)
  pf_norm_/2           - piece shifted to bounding-box origin (reused)
  pf_variants_/2       - the four quarter-turn rotations (reused)
  pf_solve_/3          - N-by-N exact-cover tiling search (reused)
  pf_render_/3         - placed cells painted as the output square (reused)
  arc2_bg_color_/2     - most common cell value as background (reused)
  arc2_transform(palette_jigsaw, +Grid, -Out)
  arc2_induce_rule(+Pairs, palette_jigsaw)
