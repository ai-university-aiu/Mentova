TASK DESCRIPTION

Task 6ffbe589 presents three training pairs and one test case. Every
input is 20x20 over background 0 and every output is a smaller
square crop: 13x13, 10x10, 10x10, and 13x13 in the test. Each input
holds one main figure - a framed square wrapped in a dotted ring -
plus two or three small straight pieces lying far away from the
figure. The palettes vary per grid: ring 3 with core colors 8 and 6
in the first training input, ring 5 with core colors 3 and 4 in the
second, ring 4 with core colors 1 and 2 in the third, and ring 5
with core colors 3 and 4 again in the test. The output is the
cropped figure with its ring zone and its core block each rotated
by a commanded number of quarter turns.

RULE: dial_rotor

The glass-box rule is a five-stage procedure whose parameters are
all read off the input grid at solve time:

1. SEGMENTATION (tw_components_/2, dr_maxbbox_/2, dr_near_box_/5)
   The 8-connected foreground components are computed, and the
   component with the largest bounding box is the main figure.
   Every component with a cell within Chebyshev distance 3 of that
   bounding box merges into the crop box - this pulls in the
   detached dotted-ring pieces - and every component farther away
   is a dial piece. The merged bounding box is cropped and must be
   square.

2. DIAL READING (dr_run_/3)
   Each dial piece must be a straight single-color run. A run of
   color C with length L commands: rotate color C's layer by
   L quarter turns clockwise, that is, by L mod 4.

3. RING AND CORE SPLIT (sort/2 over the border, dr_crop_/6)
   The ring color is the unique color appearing on the crop's
   outermost layer. The ring-zone thickness K is the minimum inset
   of any non-ring-colored cell, and the core block is the central
   region inset by K.

4. ROTATION SELECTION (memberchk/2, max_list/2)
   The ring zone spins by the ring color's dial, staying put when
   the ring color has no dial. The core block spins by the largest
   dial among the colors present in the core.

5. ASSEMBLY (dr_rotn_/3, dr_row_/8)
   The output is the full crop rotated by the ring amount with the
   independently rotated core block pasted back into the center.
   The quarter-turn rotations reuse the existing rotate_90_cw,
   rotate_180, and rotate_90_ccw named rules.

INDUCTION

The induce clause dispatches early, directly after motif_shadow.
Its pre-filter requires the first training output to be a strictly
shorter square, a sparse foreground of 10 to 300 cells, and at
least two components (a figure plus at least one dial). The clause
then verifies every training pair with a forall over the transform,
so induction is pure verification.

KEY INSIGHT

The palette-variant hypothesis suggested by the task's surface
reading is a red herring: the second training grid and the test
grid share the same palette - ring 5 with core colors 3 and 4 - yet
they need different rotations, a core quarter turn in training and
a core half turn in the test. The debris pieces that look like
noise are the actual instruction stream. Their lengths are dials:
the "33" domino commands a half turn for color 3's layer, the
single "4" commands a quarter turn, and the test's five-cell bar of
ring color 5 commands five quarter turns, which is one full
revolution plus a quarter - proving the intended reading is L mod 4.
The decisive method was to brute-force (ring transform, core
transform) pairs per training pair first, and only then hunt for
the input feature that selects each pair's combination:
length-of-piece matched all four grids with zero contradictions.
