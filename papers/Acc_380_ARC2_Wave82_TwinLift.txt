TASK DESCRIPTION

Task 62593bfd presents two training pairs and one test case. The
training grids are 28x19 and 30x30 and the test grid is 25x25. Each
grid scatters four to six small shapes over a uniform background,
and the background color differs across grids: 0 in the first
training grid, 5 in the second, and 1 in the test grid. In the
output every shape whose cell count matches the cell count of at
least one other shape has floated straight up until flush with the
top edge, and every shape whose cell count is unique among all
shapes has sunk straight down until flush with the bottom edge.
Every shape keeps its columns, its internal form, and its colors.

RULE: twin_lift

The glass-box rule is a five-stage census-and-shift procedure with
no learned parameters - the whole transformation is determined by
the grid:

1. BACKGROUND (tw_background_/2, tw_runs_/2)
   The background is the most frequent color in the grid. It is
   counted, not assumed, because the three grids use three different
   backgrounds.

2. COMPONENTS (tw_components_/2, tw_grow_/5, tw_near_/3)
   Every non-background cell is collected with its coordinates and
   color, and the cells are grouped into 8-connected components by
   greedy flood fill. Connectivity ignores color, so a shape that
   spans two colors moves as one unit while two same-colored shapes
   apart from each other move independently.

3. SIZE CENSUS
   The cell count of every component is recorded in one list.

4. DIRECTION AND SHIFT (tw_shifted_/4)
   A component whose size occurs two or more times in the census is
   a twin and floats: it shifts up by its topmost row. A component
   whose size occurs exactly once is a loner and sinks: it shifts
   down by the grid height minus one minus its bottommost row.
   Columns never change.

5. REPAINT (tw_row_/5)
   The output grid is rebuilt row by row: cells claimed by a shifted
   component take that component's cell colors, and every other cell
   takes the background color.

INDUCTION

The induce clause dispatches early, directly after wire_relay. Its
pre-filter requires the first training pair to preserve the grid
dimensions and the first input to hold between 8 and 150 foreground
cells forming at least three components, among them at least one
twin-sized pair and at least one unique-sized shape. The clause then
verifies every training pair with a forall over the transform, so
induction is pure verification.

KEY INSIGHT

The reference description of this task speaks of ranking colors by
dominance and shifting each color's shapes to a rank-determined
target. The test grid falsifies every per-color reading: it holds
two color-3 shapes - a 12-cell ring with corner dots and a 7-cell
staircase - that move in opposite directions. The direction decision
is therefore per shape. During grid analysis, positional-swap,
nearest-edge, farthest-edge, mirror-symmetry, rotational-symmetry,
density, protrusion-direction, and heavy-row hypotheses each failed
at least one of the fifteen shapes visible across the three grids.
The twin-size census is the only mechanism found that fits all
fifteen: shapes 7 and 7 rise while 5 and 11 sink in the first
training grid; 3, 3, 9, and 9 rise while 4 and 5 sink in the second;
7 and 7 rise while 6, 11, and 12 sink in the test grid.

VERIFICATION

arc2_induce_rule/2 followed by arc2_transform/3. A full regression

METHODOLOGY

Pure induction from training examples only. No pretraining on the
ARC-AGI-2 corpus. Named glass-box rules for every solved task.
