TASK DESCRIPTION

Task cbebaa4b presents 22x22 training grids and a 26x26 test grid,
input and output always the same size. The input scatters five to
seven gadget pieces over the background - each piece an 8-connected
component of one body color carrying color-2 connector studs on its
boundary. The output is the single connected machine assembled by
sliding the pieces together, without rotation, so that every stud
coincides exactly with its partner stud on another piece. The color-2
cell count halves from input to output - 16 to 8, 16 to 8, and 24 to
12 across the three grids - because paired studs merge into one cell,
while every other color keeps its exact cell count.

RULE: gadget_assembly

The glass-box named rule is a five-stage translation-only assembly
procedure with no learned parameters:

1. PIECE EXTRACTION (arc2_bg_color_/2, pf_cells_/3, pf_comps_/2)
   The background is the most common cell value. All non-background
   cells are grouped into 8-connected components over mixed colors,
   reusing the Wave 76 extraction engine unmodified. At least two
   pieces must emerge.

2. STUD READING (ga_prep_/4, ga_dir_/4)
   Each piece's color-2 cells are its connector studs. A stud's
   outward facing direction is read from its unique 4-adjacent
   non-stud cell of the same piece: the stud points away from its
   body neighbor. A stud without a unique body neighbor keeps an
   unconstrained direction.

3. BACKTRACKING STUD-PAIRING SEARCH (ga_loop_/8, ga_opp_/2)
   Piece 1 seeds the assembly at translation (0,0). The search
   repeatedly takes the first open stud and tries every stud of every
   unplaced piece as its mate. The mate must face the opposite
   direction, and the whole piece is translated by the delta that
   brings the two studs into exact coincidence. The search succeeds
   when every piece is placed and no open stud remains; any dead end
   backtracks.

4. COLLISION-CHECKED MERGING (ga_merge_/8)
   A translated piece merges into the assembly only if every cell
   that collides with an already placed cell is a stud landing on an
   open stud with opposite facing. Each such coincidence closes both
   studs and records a connection edge between the two pieces; a body
   cell colliding with anything rejects the placement.

5. GRAPH-CENTER ANCHORING AND RENDER (ga_center_/3, ga_ecc_/3,
   ga_bfs_/5, ga_render_/5)
   The recorded edges form the connection graph of the machine.
   Breadth-first search gives each piece's eccentricity, and the
   center piece - the one with minimum eccentricity - is translated
   back to its exact input position, carrying the whole assembly
   with it. The machine is painted on a fresh background canvas of
   the input's dimensions, and every assembled cell must land inside
   the grid bounds.

THE KEY INSIGHT: THE ANCHOR IS A GRAPH-THEORETIC ROLE

Wave 76 anchored its assembly on a colored stud and Wave 77 on a
role-bearing palette piece. Wave 78 generalizes the anchor once more,
to a purely graph-theoretic role: the piece that keeps its input
position is the center of the connection graph. In all three grids
the graph is a simple path - 5, 5, and 7 pieces long - so the center
is unique, and in all three grids it happens to be the color-4 piece,
but the rule reads the center from the graph structure, not from any
color. The human-steps description (gadget assembly by connector
pairing and translation) matched the data faithfully; the one detail
it left open - where the assembled machine lands on the canvas - is
exactly what the graph-center anchor answers.

INDUCTION PRE-FILTER

The first training pair must have equal input and output dimensions,
the input must carry at least two color-2 cells, the output must
carry exactly half as many color-2 cells as the input, and every
other color must keep its exact cell count. This halved stud census
is constant-time and no other task type shares it. All training pairs
are then verified with forall before the rule is selected. The induce
clause dispatches early, directly after palette_jigsaw, ahead of the
generic named-rule search.

KEY PREDICATES

  ga_prep_/4           - piece occupancy cells and open studs with facings
  ga_dir_/4            - stud outward direction from its unique body neighbor
  ga_opp_/2            - two stud facings are compatible for pairing
  ga_loop_/8           - backtracking place-one-piece-per-open-stud search
  ga_merge_/8          - collision check that pairs coinciding studs
  ga_center_/3         - connection graph center by minimum eccentricity
  ga_ecc_/3            - piece eccentricity in the connection graph
  ga_bfs_/5            - breadth-first search recording maximum depth
  ga_render_/5         - assembled cells painted on a background canvas
  arc2_bg_color_/2     - most common cell value as background (reused)
  pf_cells_/3          - non-background cells as row-column-color (reused)
  pf_comps_/2          - 8-connected mixed-color components (reused)
  arc2_transform(gadget_assembly, +Grid, -Out)
  arc2_induce_rule(+Pairs, gadget_assembly)
