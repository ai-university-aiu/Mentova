Task da515329 was one of the last hard construction tasks on the
board. The input shows almost nothing - a single small plus sign
of one color, floating on an otherwise empty background - and the
output is a dense, whole-grid spiral. The wave shows that this
enormous expansion is the deterministic unfolding of one tiny
seed under a single arm-length parameter.

TASK DESCRIPTION

Every grid holds one foreground object: a plus (a cross) drawn in
a single color on a uniform background, with an EMPTY center cell.
The plus has four equal arms; the arm length L is the number of
cells from the center to a tip. The output keeps the grid size
and the two colors, and fills the entire grid with an interlocking
spiral in the seed color.

Read as a machine, the plus is a pinwheel hub. Each of its four
arms is the starting tail of a square spiral that winds outward,
always turning the same way (clockwise). The four spirals are
ninety-degree rotations of one another, so as they wind out their
walls mesh together like the teeth of four gears, leaving exactly
a one-cell gap everywhere - a period-two pinwheel that tiles the
whole board.

THE RULE

Find the background (the most common color) and the single seed
color. The plus bounding box gives the center (its midpoint) and
the arm length L (its half-span). Three lengths follow from L:
the outward run fr = max(1, L-1), the sideways jog
jog = max(0, L-2), and the trailing run small = max(2, L).

Each arm is a turtle. It starts at the seed cell one step from the
center, walks fr cells outward to the tip, and turns ninety
degrees clockwise. Then it repeats layers k = 0, 1, 2, ...: walk
a leading run of 2 + 4k cells, jog max(0, L-2) cells sideways in
the clockwise direction, walk a trailing run of max(2, L) cells,
and turn ninety degrees clockwise. Cells that leave the grid are
simply not painted. Four such turtles, one per arm, paint the
whole pinwheel.

THE DISCRIMINATING OBSERVATION

L is a single hidden parameter that controls both the jog and the
trailing run. Because jog = max(0, L-2) is zero when L is 1 or 2,
the two smaller training pairs have perfectly straight spiral
sides and look like an ordinary interlocking spiral - a first
model of simple runs 1, 4, 8, 12, ... reproduces them. Only the
L = 3 training pair reveals the truth: its spiral sides are
stepped, carrying a one-cell jog and a trailing run that grows
with L. The unified turtle - fr = max(1, L-1), jog = max(0, L-2),
small = max(2, L), leading run 2 + 4k - is the only law that
reproduces all three training pairs AND the held-out test, whose
plus has arm length L = 4 and whose spiral sides jog by two.

IMPLEMENTATION

The rule is a new self-contained block in arc_benchmark_2.pl: the
transform arc2_transform(pinwheel_spiral)/3, the induction clause
arc2_induce_rule(pinwheel_spiral) with a single-plus-seed
pre-filter and exact training verification, and fifteen ps_
helpers - ps_seed_, ps_nonbg_, ps_cell_, ps_majority_, ps_count_,
ps_arms_, ps_arm_, ps_cw_, ps_layers_, ps_walk_, ps_line_,
ps_coord_set_, ps_render_, ps_row_, and ps_pick_. The turtle uses
only integer arithmetic and stock list predicates; grid clipping
is free because the renderer paints only the in-grid cells of a
sorted coordinate set. No existing predicate was modified and no
other rule was touched. Every code line carries an ERC comment.
