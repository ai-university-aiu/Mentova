TASK DESCRIPTION

Task a6f40cea presents three training pairs and one test case. The
inputs are 20x20, 30x30, 22x22, and 24x24 grids and the outputs are
small crops: 4x7, 13x14, 7x8, and 8x10. Each input holds one large
rectangular frame of a single color whose interior is uniformly
filled - with the background color in two grids and with a distinct
fill color in the others - plus several partial rectangle outlines
drawn outside the frame. The outlines visibly slide under the
frame: their edges break off at the frame boundary and, in one
grid, re-emerge on the far side. Two grids include outlines whose
edges alternate two colors cell by cell. The output is the frame
interior with every hidden outline cell restored, as if the frame
were transparent - an x-ray of the occluded region.

RULE: xray_frame

The glass-box rule is a five-stage procedure whose parameters are
all read off the input grid at solve time:

1. FRAME DETECTION (xr_frame_/6, xr_ring_/7)
   The majority color is the background. Candidate frames are
   single-color 8-connected components whose cells form exactly the
   perimeter of their bounding box (at least 3x3) and whose bounding
   box interior is uniformly one color. The largest candidate by
   bounding-box area is the occluding frame; smaller perfect boxes
   elsewhere in the grid remain ordinary shapes.

2. SHAPE COLLECTION (xr_shapes_/7, tw_components_/2)
   Every non-background cell strictly outside the frame's bounding
   box is a visible shape cell. The cells group into 8-connected
   components, and components sharing an identical color set merge
   into one shape - this reunites outline halves that the frame
   split apart, such as a long rectangle entering the frame at the
   top and leaving at the bottom.

3. OUTLINE COMPLETION (xr_hidden_/9)
   Each shape's bounding box is completed through the frame as if
   the frame were transparent: every perimeter cell of the shape's
   bounding box that falls strictly inside the frame interior is a
   hidden cell to restore.

4. COLOR RESTORATION (xr_restore_/13, xr_visible_/9, xr_swap_/3)
   A single-color shape restores its own color. A two-color
   alternating shape restores the color-swapped value of a mirror
   source inside its own bounding box: the hidden cell reflects to
   the opposite edge (vertical mirror first, horizontal mirror as
   fallback), the visible color there is read, and the value is
   swapped within the shape's two-color palette.

5. ASSEMBLY (xr_out_cell_/5)
   The output is the frame interior cropped from the input with the
   restored cells overlaid on the uniform fill.

INDUCTION

The induce clause dispatches early, directly after dial_rotor. Its
pre-filter requires the first training output to be strictly
shorter and strictly narrower than its input, requires a frame with
a uniform interior in the first input, and requires the output
dimensions to equal the frame interior dimensions. The clause then
verifies every training pair with a forall over the transform, so
induction is pure verification.

KEY INSIGHT

The two-color alternating outlines are a trap for every
positional-parity reading. Every visible cell of every two-color
shape in all four grids is consistent with a plain (row + column)
checkerboard, and the checkerboard correctly restores three of the
four two-color shapes - but the 9/8 shape in the third training
pair demands the exact anti-checkerboard in its hidden cells.
Plain alternation continuation, perimeter-path alternation,
border-skipping alternation, 180-degree rotation, and periodic
translation all fail on at least one shape. The only completion
that explains every hidden cell of every two-color shape is the
color-swapped mirror: reflect the hidden cell to the opposite edge
of the shape's own bounding box, read the visible color there, and
swap it within the shape's palette. Writing out every hidden cell
of every training pair by hand before coding exposed both the
contradiction and its resolution.

VERIFICATION

All three training pairs and the test case pass in SWI-Prolog under
arc2_induce_rule/2 followed by arc2_transform/3. A full regression
of all 82 locally verified tasks (81 prior plus a6f40cea) still
induces and passes with zero regressions. Implementation: nine
xr_* helper predicates and the xray_frame transform at the end of
arc_benchmark_2.pl, with one explanatory comment above every code
line. The rule passed the single-task test and the full regression
on the first compile.

METHODOLOGY

Pure induction from training examples only. No pretraining on the
ARC-AGI-2 corpus. Named glass-box rules for every solved task.
