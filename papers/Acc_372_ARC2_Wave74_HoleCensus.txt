TASK DESCRIPTION

Task 8698868d presents grids from 20x15 to 30x30 holding two families of
rectangular objects over a background. The first family is a set of
equally sized large tiles of distinct colors packed side by side into a
row-and-column layout. The second family is an equal number of smaller
rectangular shapes of their own colors scattered elsewhere over the
background. Both families are pierced by background-colored holes: the
tiles carry a few scattered single-cell holes, while the shapes carry
larger hole patterns that clump into blocks, bars, and diagonals. In the
output the scattered shapes are gone and the tile layout alone remains,
each tile redrawn solid in its own color with one of the small shapes
stamped dead center inside it, the shape's holes filled by the tile
color.

RULE: hole_census

The glass-box named rule is a five-stage pairing procedure with no
learned parameters:

1. BACKGROUND AND COMPONENTS (arc2_bg_color_/2, bst_components_/2)
   The background is the most common cell value. Every non-background
   cell is collected and partitioned into same-color 4-connected
   components.

2. CENSUS ANNOTATION (hcs_items_/4, hcs_census_/7)
   Each component is annotated with its bounding box and its hole
   census: the number of 4-connected components formed by the
   background cells inside its bounding box.

3. TILE AND SHAPE SPLIT
   The components whose bounding-box area equals the maximum are the
   tiles; all remaining components are the shapes. The tile count must
   equal the shape count, every tile must share one bounding-box size,
   and every shape must share one strictly smaller size.

4. CENSUS PAIRING (hcs_pairing_/3, hcs_match_/3)
   Each tile is paired with the unique shape whose hole census equals
   the tile's own hole census. Exactly one shape may match each tile,
   and the full pairing must use every shape exactly once - a
   verified bijection.

5. RENDER (hcs_render_/14, hcs_cell_/14, hcs_tile_cell_/10)
   The output spans exactly the packed tile layout, anchored at the
   smallest tile top row and left column. Each tile is redrawn solid
   in its own color, its paired shape is stamped centered inside it,
   shape cells keep the shape color, and shape holes take the tile
   color.

THE KEY INSIGHT: THE HOLES ARE A CENSUS

The decisive observation is that the tiles' scattered single background
holes are not noise - they are a count. A tile with three holes belongs
to the shape whose background-hole region forms exactly three
4-connected components. Two simpler readings both fail. Counting raw
hole cells fails because a shape's holes clump: a 3x3 block of nine
hole cells is one component, and its partner tile carries one hole, not
nine. Matching hole positions fails because the tile holes sit at
arbitrary spots that do not align with the shape's hole pattern when
the shape is centered. Only the component count of the holes survives
the trip from shape interior to tile face. The second observation is
that the pairing must be verified as a bijection: requiring exactly one
census match per tile and all matched shapes distinct turns any
ambiguous grid into a clean rule failure instead of a wrong stamp.

INDUCTION PRE-FILTER

The first training pair's output must have strictly fewer rows and
strictly fewer columns than its input; all training pairs are then
verified with forall before the rule is selected. The dimension check
is constant-time, keeping the pre-filter cheap for every other task in
the named-rule enumeration. The induce clause dispatches early,
directly after lattice_stamp, ahead of the generic named-rule search.

KEY PREDICATES

  hcs_items_/4         - annotate components with bounding box and census
  hcs_census_/7        - count hole components inside a bounding box
  hcs_pairing_/3       - bijective tile-shape pairing by equal census
  hcs_match_/3         - the unique shape carrying a given census
  hcs_render_/14       - paint the packed layout with centered shapes
  bst_components_/2    - same-color 4-connected components (reused)
  hc_bbox4_/5          - component bounding box (reused)
  arc2_bg_color_/2     - most common cell value as background (reused)
  arc2_transform(hole_census, +Grid, -Out)
  arc2_induce_rule(+Pairs, hole_census)
