PART 1: WHAT "UNDERSTOOD FACTS" VERSUS "REFERENCE LIBRARY" MEANS

A word is defined the first time it is used.

Causal Relation Object (CRO): the fundamental unit of Causalontology, a reified cause-and-effect relation carrying causes, effects, a temporal window, a modality, a strength, a context, and a provenance.

Node_fact: one grounded fact stored in the lattice (the "understood notebook").

The Mentova Knowledge Storage Policy splits knowledge into two tiers:

• The understood notebook is the grounded lattice of facts Mentova truly understands and can justify with a "why?"; it stays small and fast.

• The look-it-up library is the large reference bodies Mentova consults one page at a time but has not yet understood; it stays separate and is streamed line by line.

This import respects that split exactly. The word walls and the standards headings are clean enough to understand now, so they became node_facts and CROs. The thousands of lesson PDFs are bulk text that must not be asserted as fact unread, so they went to the Reference Library.

PART 4: THE DATA STRUCTURE REPORT

4.1 THE UNDERSTOOD FACTS (node_facts)

Each understood fact has the shape:

  ci_fact(Grade, Relation, Args, source(SourceId, Line)).

The final field is the citation: a source identifier and a one-based line number that resolve, through the Reference Library, to the exact line of real text the fact came from. This is testimonial grounding, and it is what lets Mentova answer "why?" honestly instead of bluffing.

When the lattice is running, curriculum_lattice anchors each ci_fact into the lattice nexus locus://mentova/curriculum as a node_fact whose referents are [grade(Grade), source(SourceId, Line)]. Verified: all 2,372 facts anchor.

Relations used, with counts:

• vocabulary  (1,850 facts) - Args = [Word, Category]. A word the grade level teaches, and the themed group it belongs to. Example: ci_fact('toddler', 'vocabulary', ['mama', 'people'], source('wall_toddler', 52)).

• math_domain  (18 facts) - Args = [DomainName, Code]. A CCSS Mathematics domain for a grade, with its official code. Example: ci_fact('kindergarten', 'math_domain', ['counting and cardinality', 'K.CC'], source('ccss_math', 557)).

• ela_strand  (6 facts) - Args = [StrandName]. A CCSS English Language Arts strand present across the Kindergarten-to-Grade-5 band (reading literature, reading informational text, reading foundational skills, writing, speaking and listening, language).

• ptklf_strand  (70 facts) - Args = [Domain, StrandId, StrandName]. A named strand within a PTKLF domain.

• ptklf_foundation  (428 facts) - Args = [Domain, FoundationId, Name]. A specific PTKLF learning foundation. Example: ci_fact('preschool_tk', 'ptklf_foundation', ['mathematics', '1.1', 'reciting numbers'], source('ptklf_math', ...)).

4.2 THE SOUND CAUSAL RELATION OBJECTS (CROs)

Each sound relation has the shape:

  ci_cro(Grade, makes_sound, Subject, Sound, source(SourceId, Line)).

These come from the "X says Y" lines of the toddler word wall (dog says woof, cat says meow, cow says moo, duck says quack, car says beep). When the lattice is running, each becomes a reified CRO of the form:

  cro(Id, [makes(Subject)], [sound(Sound)], temporal(0,0,instant), sufficient, 0.9, [grade(Grade)], prov(curriculum, source(SourceId,Line), 0.9))

That is a genuine cause-and-effect object: the cause is the subject making a sound, the effect is the sound, the strength is high, and the provenance cites the exact source line. Verified: the dog-makes-woof CRO is created and queryable.

Total sound CROs: 5.

PART 5: HOW MENTOVA CHAT REACHES THE DATA

The query interface (in curriculum_lattice.pl):

• ci_word(Grade, Word) - a word taught at a grade.
• ci_words_for_grade(Grade, Words) - a grade's whole vocabulary.
• ci_grade_of_word(Word, Grades) - where a word is taught.
• ci_standard(Grade, Domain, Code) - a CCSS mathematics domain for a grade.
• ci_ptklf(Domain, FoundationId, Name) - a PTKLF foundation.
• ci_sound(Subject, Sound) - a learned sound relation.
• ci_search(Query, Hits) - streamed full-text search across every registered source, including the bulk corpus manifest.
• ci_why(Word, Citation, Text) - the honest reason a word is known: its cited source line, read back verbatim.
• ci_cite(Citation, Text) - resolve any citation to its exact line.

At startup mc_chat_main calls ci_chat_bootstrap, which registers the sources and anchors the facts, all guarded so a curriculum problem can never crash the chat.

See the companion file Chat_on_Elementary_School_topics.txt for sample questions and expected behavior per grade level.
