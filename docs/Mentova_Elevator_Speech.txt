MENTOVA'S ELEVATOR SPEECH

From Appendix T of Teach_Mentova_To_Chat_v2 (2026-07-10).

The short, spoken description of the project, in three lengths.


THE ONE-SENTENCE VERSION

Mentova is a chatbot that only says what it can actually explain, learning language honestly from the ground up like a child, so it never bluffs.


THE THIRTY-SECOND VERSION

Mentova is an honest artificial mind that learns language the way a child does, from the ground up.

Most chatbots are fluent guessers that can sound sure while being wrong.

Mentova is different: every word it uses is tied back to simpler words it already understands, all the way down to things it can point at or learned from a trusted source, so it can always explain why it said something, and it honestly says "I do not know" instead of bluffing.

We teach it with a twenty-five level curriculum, from toddler words up to scholarly ideas, and it can talk with people through a simple web chat.


THE SIXTY-SECOND VERSION

Mentova is a glass-box artificial mind that learns language honestly, the way a child does, one grounded word at a time.

Most of today's chatbots are fluent guessers.

They predict likely words, so they can sound completely confident while being completely wrong, and they cannot really show you why they said what they said.

Mentova takes the opposite path.

Every word it knows is connected to simpler words it already understands, down to things it can perceive or has learned from a trusted, cited source, so you can always ask "why?" and follow the reasoning to the bottom.

When it has not learned something, it tells you so, plainly, instead of making something up.

We raise it with a twenty-five level curriculum that climbs from a toddler's first words all the way to scholarly language, we let it hear the music and the feeling in how things are said, and people can talk with it through a simple, honest web chat.

Over time it can even read large libraries like an encyclopedia, but only promotes something to genuine understanding after several independent checks agree and a person signs off, so it grows in knowledge without ever losing its honesty.
