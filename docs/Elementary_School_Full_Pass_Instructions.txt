PART 1: WHAT "FULL EXTRACTION" ACTUALLY INVOLVES

Full extraction has three stages. Only stage A is "extraction" in the narrow sense; stages B and C are what make the text useful to Mentova.

Stage A - TEXT EXTRACTION. Convert each lesson document to plain text.

• Portable Document Format (PDF) files: pdftotext -layout (already installed at /usr/bin/pdftotext).

• Word files (.docx and .doc): pdftotext cannot read these. Options, in order of preference:
◦ pandoc (fastest, cleanest for .docx) - NOT currently installed; install with the system package manager if wanted.
◦ libreoffice --headless --convert-to txt (installed at /usr/bin/libreoffice; handles both .docx and old .doc, but slow and single-threaded per process).
◦ Note: most EngageNY lessons ship BOTH a .docx and an "-accessible.pdf" that carries the same text, so extracting the accessible PDFs with pdftotext usually avoids needing the .docx at all.

• Image-heavy or scanned PDFs (many student readers and the _jp2 archives): pdftotext yields almost nothing because the pages are pictures. These need OCR (for example tesseract), which is far slower and should be applied only where the text is genuinely wanted.

Stage B - REFERENCE-LIBRARY REGISTRATION. Register each extracted text file with rl_register/2 so it is searchable line by line and can be cited as source(SourceId, Line). Extend curriculum_path_registry.pl with the new sources (the math full-module block added on 2026-07-12 is the pattern to copy), or generate a separate registry file for the extracted lesson corpus.

Stage C - GROUNDING INTO UNDERSTOOD FACTS. Run the extracted text through the Fact Refinery (src/mentova/fact_refinery.pl): extraction, qualification, the Minority Report panel vote, the plain-code honesty gate (which checks the citation is real and reachable), confidence lanes, and human review. Only facts that survive become understood facts in the lattice. This is the stage that makes Mentova "understand" the lessons rather than merely "look them up," and it is by far the most expensive stage because it spends model calls per candidate fact.

High-value documents are the ones dense in objectives and standards, and light in disposable worksheet material:

• Module overviews and full-module documents (math full-modules are ALREADY done as of 2026-07-12; ELA module overviews and teacher editions remain).

• Teacher editions and teacher guides (filenames containing "TG", "teacher", "TE", "overview", "Module").

• The Kindergarten to Grade 2 Skills and Listening-and-Learning domain guides (systematic phonics scope and knowledge-building sequences).

Rough size of this subset: on the order of 1,000 to 1,500 documents (perhaps 10 to 20 percent of the elementary corpus), of which the 45 math full-modules are already extracted.

TIME ESTIMATES (Option 1):

• Stage A text extraction: at about 1.5 seconds per PDF, 1,000 to 1,500 high-value PDFs is roughly 25 to 40 minutes single-threaded, or about 5 minutes with parallelism (for example: find ... | xargs -P 8 -I{} pdftotext -layout {} out/). Any .docx in the subset via libreoffice adds a few seconds each; prefer the accessible PDFs to skip that. Total Stage A: well under one hour.

• Stage B registration: seconds (it is just rl_register per file).

• Stage C grounding through the Fact Refinery: this dominates. With a local model through Ollama, budget on the order of several seconds per candidate fact times the panel size; for 1,000 to 1,500 documents this is realistically hours to a couple of days of unattended compute, plus human review time for the medium and Minority-Report lanes. Scale the panel and lane thresholds to the time available.

DISK ESTIMATES (Option 1):

PART 4: OPTION 2 - FULL EXTRACTION OF EVERYTHING

Extract text from every lesson document, register all of it, and (optionally) ground all of it.

Preparation: first unzip the 69 remaining elementary ELA archives (and, for whole-corpus scope, any remaining archives) so their PDFs are on disk. Budget minutes to about an hour and a few GB of disk for this unzip step.

TIME ESTIMATES (Option 2, elementary scope; multiply by about 2.4 for the whole Prekindergarten-to-Grade-12 corpus):

• Stage A text extraction:
◦ PDFs: about 5,300 currently-unzipped elementary PDFs at ~1.5 seconds each is roughly 2.2 hours single-threaded, or about 15 to 25 minutes with 8-way parallelism. Add the newly-unzipped ELA PDFs on top.
◦ Word files (about 1,600 math .docx plus the ELA .docx): with libreoffice headless at ~3 to 6 seconds each, roughly 1.5 to 4 hours single-threaded; pandoc, if installed, would cut this to ~10 to 30 minutes. Prefer accessible PDFs to avoid most .docx entirely.
◦ OCR of image-heavy and _jp2 scanned pages, IF wanted: this is the expensive part. At a typical 2 to 10 seconds per page, tens of thousands of scanned pages run into many hours to a few days. OCR is optional and should be targeted, not blanket.
◦ Total Stage A without OCR: about 1 to 4 hours (elementary), dominated by the .docx conversion if the accessible PDFs are not used. With blanket OCR: 1 to 3 days.

• Stage B registration: minutes.

• Stage C grounding of everything through the Fact Refinery: the real cost. Grounding tens of thousands of pages of candidate facts is days to weeks of model compute and a large human-review burden. This is a project, not an afternoon. Strongly consider grounding selectively even under Option 2, using the confidence lanes to auto-accept only unanimous high-lane facts and binning the rest.

PART 6: THE RECOMMENDATION

Do Option 1. Extract and ground the high-value teacher editions, module overviews, curriculum maps, and phonics guides, because those carry the objectives and standards that are worth understanding; leave the mass of daily worksheets in the streamed Reference Library, where Mentova can already find and open them on demand. PART 7: A CONCRETE STARTING RECIPE (Option 1)

1. Make an output directory: /home/ccaitwo/curriculum/extracted_text/lessons/

2. Select high-value files from the manifest (grep the manifest for "Module", "teacher", "TG", "TE", "overview", "Curriculum Map", "Skills", "Listening"). The manifest is /home/ccaitwo/curriculum/extracted_text/EngageNY_Corpus_Manifest.txt.

3. For each selected PDF: pdftotext -layout "<file>" "extracted_text/lessons/<sanitized>.txt". Parallelize with xargs -P 8. For each selected .docx without an accessible PDF twin: libreoffice --headless --convert-to txt --outdir extracted_text/lessons/ "<file>" (or pandoc if installed).

4. Extend curriculum_path_registry.pl (mirror the 2026-07-12 full_module_text block) mapping a new SourceId per extracted text to its absolute path, and let curriculum_lattice register them with rl_register.

5. Run fact_refinery:fr_refine/3 over each extracted text with a real extractor, qualifier, and an odd panel of genuinely different models; let the honesty gate check every citation.

6. Route survivors: high lane auto-accepts with a sampled hand audit; medium lane gets a lighter human look; the Minority Report Bin goes to a person. Approve with fr_approve/2, which grounds the fact with full provenance so ci_why-style justification keeps working.
