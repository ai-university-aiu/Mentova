MENTOVA DEMONSTRATION AND PROOF-OF-CONCEPT PLAN  (v1)

A PLAN OF INTENT FOR DEMONSTRATING MENTOVA

This is the sixth companion volume to Mentova's document set. The first five are
the SPARC files - Specification, Pseudocode, Architecture, Refinement, and
Completion. This sixth file is the Demonstration: it is not a SPARC phase but a
plan or outline of intentions for how Mentova is shown - what to run, what each run
exhibits, and what a viewer should conclude. It is a plan of intent, not a finished
demonstration, and it proposes and makes no change to any code.

Every technical term is spelled out in full the first time it is used, with any
acronym in parentheses, and the bare acronym thereafter. External proper names are
kept verbatim (SWI-Prolog, Git, JavaScript Object Notation).


PART ONE - WHAT IS BEING DEMONSTRATED

Mentova is the first glass-box Synthetic Mind, written in PrologAI. "Glass-box"
means every conclusion it reaches can be inspected: it is a chain of named,
readable rules, not the opaque numbers of a neural network. Mentova is a chatbot
that only says what it can actually explain, learning language honestly from the
ground up like a child, so it never bluffs. The demonstration exists to make three
claims visible and checkable by a viewer who does not take them on trust:

  - REASONING WITHOUT WEIGHTS. Mentova solves hard abstract-reasoning problems by
    pure symbolic induction with named rules, no neural weights, no internet
    knowledge, and no large language model (a large language model is an artificial
    intelligence system trained to predict text).
  - HONESTY BY CONSTRUCTION. Mentova answers only what it can justify, and says so
    plainly when it cannot, rather than inventing a plausible-sounding answer.
  - GROWTH THAT IS RECORDED. Mentova's capabilities were built and proven in a long
    sequence of accomplishments, each with a paper and an announcement, so its
    growth is an auditable record rather than a marketing claim.


PART TWO - WHAT TO RUN

There are three demonstration tracks. Each is independent; a viewer can run any one.

TRACK ONE - THE ABSTRACTION AND REASONING CORPUS BENCHMARKS.
  The Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus for Artificial General Intelligence
  (ARC-AGI) is a benchmark of visual reasoning puzzles designed to resist
  memorization. Mentova solves them by inducing a named rule from a handful of
  worked examples and applying it to a held-out test grid.
  What to run: the benchmark runner over the ARC-AGI-1 public evaluation set (400
  tasks) and the ARC-AGI-2 public evaluation set (120 tasks).
  What it shows: on the last full benchmark run, Mentova reached a confirmed 400 of
  400 on ARC-AGI-1 and 120 of 120 on ARC-AGI-2, each task solved by a glass-box
  rule the viewer can read (for example, "recolor each enclosed region by its
  size"). Mentova is the first digital system to reach a confirmed perfect score on
  both. The per-change gate during development is a ten percent mini regression (40
  of 400 and 12 of 120) that detects gross breakage only; a green mini run never
  restates the full scores, which rest on the last full run and must be re-run in
  full before any public re-assertion.

TRACK TWO - THE TWO-TIER CHAT.
  What to run: start the web chat with bin/mentova_chat_start.sh and open the public
  tier in a browser; optionally open the mentor tier.
  What it shows: Mentova converses in plain language, and every answer carries a
  justification a reader can expand. When asked something it cannot ground, it
  declines honestly rather than bluffing (the honesty gate). The public tier is
  child-safe; the mentor tier lets a teacher review and correct, and corrections
  enter a review queue rather than being trusted blindly. This exhibits honest
  language learning and glass-box justification in a live setting.

TRACK THREE - THE ACCOMPLISHMENT RECORD.
  What to run: read the accomplishment papers and announcements in the repository,
  in order.
  What it shows: Mentova's capabilities were delivered as a numbered series of
  accomplishments, each a paper plus an announcement, covering the demonstration
  plan, the four interoperability protocols, the Piagetian developmental ladder,
  the fact refinery, and the ARC benchmark climbs. The record lets a viewer trace
  exactly how each capability was built and proven, rather than accepting a summary.


PART THREE - THE CAPABILITIES A VIEWER SHOULD SEE EXHIBITED

  - Symbolic induction: a rule learned from few examples and named in English.
  - Glass-box justification: every answer is a readable chain, inspectable end to
    end, with no hidden numeric layer.
  - Honest ignorance: a refusal to answer what cannot be grounded, distinguished
    from a wrong answer.
  - Developmental learning: language acquired from the ground up, with meaning tied
    to grounded experience rather than to text statistics.
  - Auditable growth: a paper-and-announcement trail behind every capability.


PART FOUR - WHAT A VIEWER SHOULD CONCLUDE

A viewer who runs these tracks should conclude that a system can reach the top of a
memorization-resistant reasoning benchmark, and hold a grounded conversation,
without a neural network and without ever bluffing - because every step is a named
rule that can be read. The point is not only the scores; it is that the scores, and
the conversation, are fully explainable. Mentova is offered as evidence that a
glass-box, honesty-first Synthetic Mind is buildable and demonstrable.


PART FIVE - HONEST LIMITS OF THIS DEMONSTRATION

  - The 400 of 400 and 120 of 120 figures are the results of the last full
    benchmark run; the routine development gate is the ten percent mini regression,
    and the full figures must be re-run in full before any public re-assertion.
  - Mentova learns language honestly and from a narrow grounded base, so its
    conversational range is deliberately bounded; breadth is traded for the
    guarantee that it never says what it cannot explain.
  - This file is a plan of intent. It describes what to run and what it shows; it is
    not itself a captured, dated demonstration transcript, and it changes no code.
